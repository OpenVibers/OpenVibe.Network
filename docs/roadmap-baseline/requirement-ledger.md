## How a row is checked

Status is one of: **met**: the §15.0 required target is demonstrated by current evidence: a test that exercises it, and production evidence where the target is a production authority or state. **partial**: some of the target is demonstrated and the next steps are agent-doable; an owner item may also be named. **not met**: essentially nothing of the target is demonstrated. **blocked on owner**: the core of the target cannot be demonstrated until the owner acts (the action is named); agent-doable items that remain are listed too.


Every run re-verifies each artifact: a test, doc or drill file must exist at origin/main and contain the quoted text; a commit must be on origin/main; a snapshot check is re-evaluated against `data/prod-snapshot.json`; a production observation must record its command, result and time, and must be less than 14 days older than the snapshot. A row is **UNVERIFIED** when any artifact fails, when it is met without a test, production check or drill (roadmap §8.3: a route, table, page, doc or commit alone is not proof), when it is met but lists remaining work, when it is not met but lists nothing remaining, or when it is blocked on the owner without naming the owner action.
