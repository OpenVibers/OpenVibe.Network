Your Accounts

Switch between multiple accounts, Google-style. You can have up to 5 accounts logged in at once.

Merge two accounts

Own two accounts? Fold one into the other. Its follows, OpenCoins, linked sign-ins, sessions and settings move to the account you keep, which keeps its name and profile. The other account can't be used on its own afterwards: signing in to it signs you in to the one you kept. If it was a mistake, support can split it by hand within 30 days.

Download your data

Get a copy of what OpenVibe keeps about you: your account here, and your part of Live, Chat, Community, Media and Games, as JSON files in one .zip. Each service adds its own files, so it takes up to half an hour. You can download it for 7 days, and ask for one a day.

Delete your account

Your account is deleted 30 days after you ask, and you can cancel any time before then; until that date everything works as usual. On the day, your account here is erased and every service erases what it keeps about you: messages, posts, follows, media and settings. Replies other people wrote under your posts stay, without your name. Payment records are kept without your name, as the law requires. After that it cannot be undone, and your username becomes free for someone else.
